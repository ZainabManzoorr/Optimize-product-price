Market Intelligence & Product Price Optimization
Problem Statement

In today’s competitive electronics market, pricing intelligence plays a crucial role in capturing customer demand and maximizing profitability. Laptops, in particular, exhibit a wide range of specifications, features, and brand perceptions that directly influence their market value.

However, customers often struggle to identify price–performance tradeoffs, while businesses face challenges in setting optimal prices that balance competitiveness with profit margins.

The objective of this project is to:

Analyze how laptop attributes (RAM, storage, processor, GPU, brand, etc.) affect market price.

Study customer-centric factors like ratings and demand to understand consumer perception.

Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to identify key patterns and correlations.

Build a foundation for price optimization models that could assist companies in strategic pricing decisions.

This project combines market intelligence with predictive analytics to simulate how businesses can make data-driven pricing decisions.

📊 Dataset

The dataset is a synthetic but realistic laptop dataset curated for market intelligence and price optimization tasks.

Key Features:

Brand – Laptop manufacturer (Dell, HP, Lenovo, Apple, etc.)

Processor – Type of CPU (i5, i7, Ryzen, M1, etc.)

GPU – Graphics card used (Integrated, NVIDIA, AMD, etc.)

RAM_GB – RAM capacity (numeric, in GB)

Storage_GB – Storage size (numeric, in GB)

Launch_Year – Year of release

Customer_Rating – Average customer rating (1–5 scale)

Demand_Index – Simulated demand score (proxy for popularity)

Competitor_Price – Price of closest competitor model

Price – Final selling price of the laptop (target variable)

Size & Format:

File type: CSV

Rows: ~1,000 (simulated entries)

Columns: 10 (features + target)

This dataset is structured to allow:

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) – brand-level trends, feature-price relationships.

Correlation & Feature Engineering – creation of metrics like Performance Index and Discount Factor.

Predictive Modeling – regression or ML-based price optimization.
